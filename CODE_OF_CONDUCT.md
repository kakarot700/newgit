# Code of Conduct

NewGit is a technical project where people should be able to collaborate respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in discussions and reviews.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence—not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and welcoming.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub's contact/reporting tools. Include links and context, and avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly to the extent possible.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the NewGit project.
